# v6-3 - Night Lid / Vine

## Thesis

A loose botanical trellis climbing the wall, the sparsest of the three.
This is [v6 Night Lid](../v6/DIRECTION.md) with exactly one thing changed: the pattern printed into the box stock.
The grid-and-circles motif read as graph paper under the pieces; a tapestry repeat reads as a dim room the box is sitting in.
Everything else - palette, type, pieces, the die-cut spy, the countdown ring - is v6 unchanged, so the three variants are a fair test of the pattern alone.

## The pattern

A 96 px repeat: a stem running corner to corner with a paired leaf either side of it and a berry at the crossing, plus a second stem on the opposite diagonal held back to 45% so the trellis reads as depth rather than as a grid.
Sparse on purpose - it leaves the largest quiet areas of any of the three, which is what a screen carrying a long roster wants behind it.

## Ink

Brass at 9.5% on the lid and 7% on the board, over the same 8 px twill.
The pattern sits at `z-index: -1` inside the stock, under every piece printed on it, so a die-cut button or the insert always covers it rather than blending with it.

## The card is untouched

The card back keeps v6's own print - the single brass lozenge on aubergine with a foil corner pip in each corner.
A card is held close to one face and read in a hurry, so its surface stays clean; the tapestry belongs to the room, not to the thing being passed around.

## Risk

A repeat this large can look like wallpaper stock photography if it is ever brightened.
It only works because it stays under 10% brass and never crosses a piece.
