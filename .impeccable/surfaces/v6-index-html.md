## Direction contract

THESIS: The same box, but the deluxe night edition — a dark aubergine lid, hot-foil stamped in brass, linen-embossed, with the spy's die-cut token as its box art.
v2 was the box on the table in daylight; v6 is the box in the room the game is actually played in.

OWN-WORLD: Lid aubergine `#241238` grounds home, discussion and ended; near-black plum `#150c22` is the punchboard for the setup screens, with a raised violet-plum insert `#33204d` printed on it.
Brass foil `#e8b34a` is the single accent mass and the only primary action colour, with deep plum `#1a0d29` as its ink; ember `#e0503c` is reserved for the spy card face, the caret and error ink; violet `#6b46a8` is the punched token stock.
Die-cut pieces keep v2's 4 px bottom edge and press-to-sink; disabled still means "never punched out of the sheet".
Every ground carries three real layers: a linen emboss grid, the die-cut token motif, and a fibre noise tile, plus a vignette so the frame reads as an object.

STORY: A group that owns the daylight box recognises the shelf, then sees this is the edition worth bringing out after dark.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The lid. A 208 px die-cut spy token top-right — a fedora-and-collar bust cropped by the punch circle, brass-outlined, two brass eye-slivers under the brim — then "Spy" at 112 px in Rammetto One, white, with a brass foil second impression offset 5 px, the brass rule, the tagline, and the single full-width brass "Play Spy" piece at the thumb.

SIGNATURE: The discussion clock. A 244 px token with a brass ring stroked around its rim; the ring is a full circle at 5:00 and the gap opens at twelve o'clock and grows clockwise as the round burns down, gliding one second at a time.

FORM: The board-game box, night edition; a remix of v2, not a new world.
Honest risk: three dark purples separated by small value steps is a narrow band to build legibility in, and the brief's own "darker" pushes toward the flat-dark-app look that v2 was built to refuse.

FINISH: unreviewed is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review and its verdict.
