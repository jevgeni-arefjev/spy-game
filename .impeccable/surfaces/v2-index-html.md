# v2 — Box Lid

Scope: the whole Spy flow as a static mockup, `v2/index.html`, seven frames per `.impeccable/surfaces/mockup-harness.md`.
Mode: Operate.
Audience, job, constraints: see PRODUCT.md; nothing pinned by the user beyond "game night vibe, go wide".
Kind: Impeccable's pick (top-ranked grounded candidate, 1 of 7).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is the board-game box already on the table: thick matte cardboard, spot-varnished title, die-cut punchboard tokens.
It refuses the flat "party app" and the dark spy-noir alike; it is a physical product you could pick up.

OWN-WORLD: Full palette from the box print: lid teal `#0e5e5c` as the ground of the home, discussion and ended screens; kraft cardboard `#c9a97e` with cream printed inserts `#f4ead6` for the setup screens (topics, players); tomato red `#d9432f` for the one primary token per screen; mustard `#e8b62c` for the spy face; card-back pattern in teal on cream.
Display face Rammetto One for the title, the word and the clock; Rubik (400–700) for every other label.
Controls are die-cut pieces: rounded rectangles with a 4 px darker bottom edge that reads as cardboard thickness; a press sinks the piece by that edge.
Topics are round punch tokens: included tokens sit punched out and raised with a soft offset shadow, excluded tokens still sit in the sheet, flat, with the die-cut outline visible.
Players are name tiles in a rack.
The face-down card is a real card with the teal pattern back; tapping flips it.
Disabled: still in the sheet (flat outline, no thickness).

STORY: A first-timer sees a game they already know how to hold; a regular sees a box worth bringing out again.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home is the lid: teal ground edge to edge, "Spy" in Rammetto One at roughly 112 px in cream with a red spot-varnish offset, the tagline in Rubik 20 px cream below, a repeated die-cut token motif faint in the teal, and the single red "Play Spy" piece at the bottom, full width, 60 px tall.

FORM: The board-game box and its punchboard; grounded candidate 1 of 7 (Impeccable's pick, not the assignment); seed key a9839155.
Honest risk: familiar; this is close to where the category (Codenames, Ticket to Ride) already lives, and the pick card says so.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
