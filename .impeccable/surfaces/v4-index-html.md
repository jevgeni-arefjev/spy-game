## Direction contract

THESIS: Every screen is a record sleeve: one enormous condensed word shouts through immaculate information noise.
The word that matters (SPY, TOPICS, PRIYA, HOT-AIR BALLOON, 03:47, TIME'S UP) never shrinks; everything else is a small official-looking mark at the edge.
It refuses both the noir dossier and the friendly rounded app.

OWN-WORLD: Absolute black `#000`, magenta `#ff2d95`, safety orange `#ff5a00`, white `#fff`; flat print, no gradients, no shadows, no rounded corners.
Shouting face Sofia Sans Extra Condensed 800–900 in caps; everything else Sofia Sans 600 caps at 11–13 px, tracked.
The noise layer is built only from true facts: registration marks, a product-code strip that reads the real state (`ROUND 01 · PLAYERS 06 · SPIES 01 · TOPICS 03/05`), a barcode built from the roster count, and the shipped Russian locale strings (ШПИОН, ТЕМЫ, ИГРОКИ, ВРЕМЯ ВЫШЛО) as the second-language ornament, in place of faux-kanji, because this product really ships in Russian.
Controls are stamped plates: the primary action a solid magenta plate with black caps; secondary a white hairline plate; active states add another mark (an included topic gains a magenta registration roundel; disabled gets a diagonal hatch).
The face-down card is a black sleeve with the name in the product-code slot; tap slides the inner sleeve out and the word shouts.
Density tiers: at 360 px only tier 1 noise ships (code strip, four corner registration marks, one Cyrillic line); the shouting word and the primary plate never lose clarity.

STORY: A regular reads it as a club flyer for tonight; a first-timer reads the one huge word and the one magenta plate and knows what to do.
