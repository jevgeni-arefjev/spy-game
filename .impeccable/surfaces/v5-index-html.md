## Direction contract

THESIS: The round is a lighting cue sequence on a cyclorama: it starts in blackout and the light rises through the phases until "Time's up" is full white day.
Colour is the material and it is light, not print; it refuses the printed-paper and the neon-glow worlds both.

OWN-WORLD: Depthless cyc black `#050507` for home (blackout); a low cobalt horizon band `#1435c8` fading upward for topics and players (night); cobalt with rose `#ff5aa0` gathering above it for handoff and card (first light); rose and amber `#ffb36b` blooming for discussion (dawn); a white day wash `#f4f2ee` with black type for ended (day), still carrying a thin cobalt line at the foot so it is the same wall.
Content is held in horizontal horizon bands stacked dark to light: topic rows and roster rows are bands, each lit from below when active.
Cue labels in Saira Stencil One caps at 11 px with tabular numerals (`LX 001 BLACKOUT`, `LX 127 NIGHT`, `LX 263 FIRST LIGHT`, `LX 389 DAWN`, `LX 512 DAY`) name the phase on every screen so colour is never the only signal; the cue number rises through the round.
Everything else in Saira (wdth 100, 400–700) with tabular figures.
Controls: RAISE is the primary action, a band that lights fully when pressed; HOLD is pause (the label reads `Pause` / `Resume` verbatim, the state word `HOLD` sits beside it); BLACKOUT is End round.
The face-down card is a black card in silhouette against the lit cyc; tapping raises the light inside the card from its bottom edge until the word is lit.
The word "Hot-air balloon" is the actor, in white against the risen light.

STORY: The group feels the round get brighter as it goes; when the screen turns white, time is up and nobody needs to read it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home is blackout: a black screen with a single thin cobalt horizon line at 62% height, "Spy" in Saira 700 at roughly 88 px in white above the line, the tagline in white 60% below it, the cue label `LX 001 BLACKOUT` top-left, and the "Play Spy" band at the bottom, full width, 60 px tall, cobalt lit.

FORM: The stagecraft cyclorama at dawn; dealt challenger; seed key a9839155.
Honest risk: gradients read as decoration unless they behave like light with a horizon and a falloff; the day wash must still be legible as the same world.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
