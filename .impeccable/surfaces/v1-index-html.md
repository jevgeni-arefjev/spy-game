## Direction contract

THESIS: The secret is a note passed under the table.
Everything a player reads or writes is pencil on ruled notebook paper; the app's own chrome is the exercise book's printed matter.
It refuses the category's two ruts: the noir spy-dossier with stamps and redaction, and the pastel party-app with confetti.

OWN-WORLD: Warm-white ruled paper `#f7f3ea` with pale-blue feint rules and one red margin line; graphite `#2a2a2e` for every handwritten word; one ballpoint blue `#1d3fbd` as the only accent, the pen that circles and fills.
Handwriting face Caveat (400–700) for names, the word, the hint, the clock digits and every button label; a small printed grotesk (Schibsted Grotesk) for exercise-book labels only.
Controls are pen-drawn boxes: an SVG rectangle with a slightly irregular stroke; the primary action is the box filled solid in ballpoint blue with the label knocked out in paper white.
The face-down card is a folded note with a visible crease and shadow; tapping unfolds it.
Selected topic: circled in ballpoint (kept from the doujin catalog's hand-marked states).
Disabled: pencil hatching over the box.
Hierarchy by scale contrast alone: the word is enormous, the labels tiny (kept from the type-specimen challenger).
Raise, named for its donor (variable-font specimen): no boxes, cards or containers beyond the note itself; whitespace and scale do the structuring.
Raise, named for its donor (doujin catalog): every state is a hand mark, circled, struck through, hatched, never a colour swap alone.

STORY: The player understands this is a private note meant only for them, keeps it low, and passes the phone on.
The group reads the clock as something scribbled on the same page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home is a mostly empty ruled page; "Spy" handwritten in pencil at roughly 96 px near the top third, the tagline in pencil below it at 28 px, and one ballpoint-filled box "Play Spy" in the lower third, full width minus the margin, 56 px tall.
